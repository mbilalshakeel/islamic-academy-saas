Wudu Guide

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَنِ الرَّحِيمِ

"In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful." — begin every Wudu with this intention.

أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا عَبْدُهُ وَرَسُولُهُ

Recite the Shahada after completing Wudu for added reward.

Sana

سُبْحَانَكَ اللَّهُمَّ وَبِحَمْدِكَ وَتَبَارَكَ اسْمُكَ

"Glory be to You, O Allah, and all praise is Yours, and blessed is Your name."

Al-Fatiha

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ ۝ الرَّحْمَنِ الرَّحِيمِ ۝ مَالِكِ يَوْمِ الدِّينِ

"Praise be to Allah, Lord of all the worlds, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful, Master of the Day of Judgment."

Tasbih of Ruku

سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْعَظِيمِ

"Glory be to my Lord, the Most Great." (recited 3 times while bowing)

Tasbih of Sajdah

سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْأَعْلَى

"Glory be to my Lord, the Most High." (recited 3 times in prostration)

Durood Ibrahim

اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَى مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ

"O Allah, send blessings upon Muhammad and the family of Muhammad." — recited in the final sitting.

1
Niyyah (Intention)

Stand straight facing the Qibla and make your intention in your heart for the specific prayer.

2
Takbiratul Ihram

Raise your hands to your ears and say "Allahu Akbar" to enter the state of prayer.

3
Qiyam (Standing)

Recite Sana, Ta'awwuz, Bismillah, Al-Fatiha, and a Surah from the Quran.

4
Ruku (Bowing)

Bow with your back straight, hands on knees, and recite "Subhana Rabbiyal Azim" three times.

5
Sajdah (Prostration)

Place forehead, nose, palms, knees, and toes on the ground and recite "Subhana Rabbiyal A'la" three times — twice with a short sitting in between.

6
Tashahhud & Salam

In the final sitting, recite At-Tahiyyat, Durood, and Dua, then turn your face right then left saying "Assalamu Alaikum wa Rahmatullah" to end the prayer.
